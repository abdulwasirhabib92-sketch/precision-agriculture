Privacy Policy

Overview

Top YouTube Auto is a personal application owned and operated by Abdul Wasir Habib. It requests access to a single Google account (the owner's own account) for the purpose of uploading and managing videos on the owner's YouTube channel.

Information We Access

With your explicit consent, the application may request the following Google account permissions:

  • YouTube upload permission — to upload videos to the connected YouTube channel
  • YouTube read permission — to view channel information such as video counts and titles

How Information Is Used

Access tokens are used solely to perform the actions you request (uploading and managing your own videos). The application:

  • Does not sell, rent, or share data with third parties
  • Does not use your data for advertising or analytics
  • Does not store your Google password (authorization uses Google's secure OAuth system)

Data Storage and Security

Authorization credentials are stored in encrypted storage accessible only to the application owner. You may revoke the application's access at any time by visiting Google Account Permissions.
